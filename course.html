Summary

In this first video, basic signs related to World Religions and Interfaith Studies are taught. You will learn how to express concepts such as prayer, meditation, sacred texts (Bible, Quran, Torah, Vedas), places of worship (church, mosque, temple, synagogue), and key figures in different religious traditions using LESSA (Salvadoran Sign Language). This video introduces fundamental vocabulary about Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism, and Indigenous spiritualities. The course promotes interfaith understanding, cultural respect, and inclusive communication for religious diversity in a clear and professional way.
